LEED values are shown for New Construction within LEED v5 BD+C; WELL point values are shown for WELL Certification (WELL Core can differ). Working process

From project information to defensible results.

A compact workflow designed for external collaboration: clear inputs, traceable assumptions and outputs that are straightforward to review.

STEP 01
→

Scope & inputs

Review drawings, BIM exports, specifications and assessor requirements. Define model boundaries, scenarios and deliverables.

STEP 02
→

Model build

Develop thermal geometry, zoning, constructions, gains, schedules, ventilation and relevant HVAC assumptions.

STEP 03
→

Simulate & compare

Run hourly simulations, design variants and future-climate cases; isolate the parameters that materially affect performance.

STEP 04

Report & handover

Provide concise calculation evidence, tables, plots, screenshots, assumptions and QA notes for project use.
