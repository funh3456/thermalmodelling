IES VE modelling support

Additional modelling capacity for engineering and sustainability teams: from drawings and project information to an organised thermal model and a clear set of results. Work can be scoped as a standalone package or as white-label support within your project team.

IES VEDefined scopeTraceable assumptions

What the service covers

  • Thermal model geometry and zoning from architectural drawings or a suitable model export.
  • Construction, occupancy, equipment, lighting and operating-schedule inputs based on the agreed brief.
  • Hourly simulation, heating and cooling load assessment, and comparison of design options.
  • Model checks, an assumptions register and an agreed handover of model files and results.

What to send with your enquiry

  • Plans, sections, elevations and the latest architectural revision.
  • Envelope data, glazing and shading information, room uses and occupancy schedules.
  • HVAC concept, set-points, ventilation assumptions and the questions the model needs to answer.

Send the information you already have. Missing inputs and the required level of detail are identified before the task is agreed.

What you receive

  • A model and input register aligned with the agreed scope.
  • Scenario comparison tables and plots with the main assumptions stated.
  • A technical summary of findings, uncertainties and decisions requiring project-team confirmation.

The exact model files, report content and review stages are defined in the agreed project brief.

How the work progresses

  1. Define the question. Agree the intended decisions, inputs, assessment criteria and deliverables.
  2. Build and check. Review geometry, schedules and model assumptions before interpreting results.
  3. Compare and explain. Report the scenarios, their differences and any inputs that still require confirmation.

Common questions

Can you work as part of our existing project team?

Yes. The package can support your team under an agreed scope and reporting format. Responsibility for inputs, review and final project decisions is agreed at the start.

Can you take over an existing IES VE model?

An existing model can be reviewed first to establish its version, completeness and suitability. The agreed scope then identifies which parts can be retained and which need updating.
