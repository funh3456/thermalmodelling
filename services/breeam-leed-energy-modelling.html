BREEAM and LEED energy modelling support

Translate the project’s agreed certification requirements into a defined modelling task. Technical modelling support helps assessor and consultant teams obtain traceable inputs, repeatable scenario comparisons and clearly documented results.

IES VEDefined scopeTraceable assumptions

What the service covers

  • Review of the modelling task against the project scheme, version and selected assessment route.
  • Energy and load simulation using the agreed baseline and proposed-design assumptions where required by the selected route.
  • Comparison of envelope, operational and system-design options within the agreed model scope.
  • Preparation of result tables, figures and an assumptions register for review by the responsible project team.

What to send with your enquiry

  • Certification scheme, registration version, assessment type and targeted requirements.
  • Architectural and building-services documentation, schedules and agreed model inputs.
  • Assessor or consultant instructions, selected compliance route and required evidence format.

Send the information you already have. Missing inputs and the required level of detail are identified before the task is agreed.

What you receive

  • A documented modelling basis distinguishing project inputs from assumptions.
  • Scenario results and technical explanations linked to the agreed assessment scope.
  • A review package identifying open inputs and evidence that must be confirmed by the responsible team.

The exact model files, report content and review stages are defined in the agreed project brief.

How the work progresses

  1. Define the question. Agree the intended decisions, inputs, assessment criteria and deliverables.
  2. Build and check. Review geometry, schedules and model assumptions before interpreting results.
  3. Compare and explain. Report the scenarios, their differences and any inputs that still require confirmation.

Common questions

Does modelling support guarantee certification points?

No. Modelling supports the evidence package. Eligibility and the final award depend on the applicable scheme, the complete project evidence and the authorised assessment process.

Can a model be used for both BREEAM and LEED?

Some project information may be reusable, but the modelling rules, baselines, versions and evidence requirements must be checked separately. A shared model does not remove those differences.
