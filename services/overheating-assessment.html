Overheating assessment and thermal comfort modelling

Test whether an occupied space is likely to become too warm, and understand which design changes improve its performance. The assessment connects hourly simulation results to practical decisions about glazing, shading, ventilation and operation.

IES VEDefined scopeTraceable assumptions

What the service covers

  • Hourly temperature and thermal comfort simulation for the agreed rooms and zones.
  • Comparison of glazing, solar shading, ventilation and operating strategies.
  • Present-day or future-climate scenarios where suitable weather data and assessment criteria are agreed.
  • Identification of sensitive assumptions and spaces that need further design attention.

What to send with your enquiry

  • Room geometry, orientation, glazing, shading and construction properties.
  • Occupancy, internal gains, opening schedules and proposed ventilation strategy.
  • Project location, weather-data requirements and the intended comfort or overheating assessment method.

Send the information you already have. Missing inputs and the required level of detail are identified before the task is agreed.

What you receive

  • Room-level results, time-series plots and summaries against the agreed assessment criteria.
  • A comparison of design options showing the assumptions used for each scenario.
  • A technical report explaining findings, limitations and recommended next design steps.

The exact model files, report content and review stages are defined in the agreed project brief.

How the work progresses

  1. Define the question. Agree the intended decisions, inputs, assessment criteria and deliverables.
  2. Build and check. Review geometry, schedules and model assumptions before interpreting results.
  3. Compare and explain. Report the scenarios, their differences and any inputs that still require confirmation.

Common questions

Does the assessment use future weather?

Future-climate scenarios can be included when the brief defines the weather source, time horizon and scenario. These inputs are documented so that results remain traceable.

Does every project use the same pass criteria?

No. The applicable assessment method depends on the building use, project requirements and any certification route. The criteria and version must be agreed before the results are interpreted.
