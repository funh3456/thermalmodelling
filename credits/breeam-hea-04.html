Certification scope/BREEAM NC V7/Hea 04
BREEAM International NC V7.1

Indoor air quality

Ventilation and IAQ design calculations, strategy and documentation support; post-construction air testing remains a separate field activity.

Hea 04Up to 5 credits + 2 exemplary credits

What the requirement covers

Hea 04 combines an IAQ plan, ventilation, construction-product emissions and post-construction air-quality measurement. Ventilation support is only one part of the issue. The selected product categories and test evidence need separate coordination with the IAQ professional and assessor.

Engineering support focuses on IAQ strategy and ventilation; product certificates and air sampling require separate evidence.

  • Full issue: up to 5 credits plus 2 exemplary product-emissions credits
  • The manual requires a suitable IAQ professional for specified activities
  • Do not infer pollutant-test compliance from design airflow calculations

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Space use, occupancy, ventilation zones and outdoor-air schedules
  • Air-handling, filtration and exhaust designs
  • Outdoor pollutant sources, intake locations and airflow paths
  • IAQ strategy, construction controls and planned testing responsibilities

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

How I support the assessment

  1. Confirm the ventilation standard, pollutants, intake / exhaust separation and filtration strategy
  2. Prepare room / system outdoor-air calculations and review CO₂ or air-quality control where required
  3. Coordinate the IAQ plan, product-emissions evidence and specialist post-construction test programme

What I deliver

  • Ventilation / filtration calculation and design-review package
  • Room / system compliance matrix with the agreed standard edition
  • IAQ evidence register and clearly separated field-testing scope

Verification and responsibilities

Design calculations do not establish actual indoor pollutant concentrations. Product-emission certificates and post-construction testing must be supplied by the responsible specialists.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
