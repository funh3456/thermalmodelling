Enhanced Commissioning

Enhanced MEP commissioning support plus enclosure-pathway airtightness testing, field verification and infrared thermography where selected.

EAc5Selected enclosure pathway: 1 pointEnvelope · Airtightness & thermography

What the requirement covers

The selected EAc5 scope is the building-enclosure commissioning pathway. The wider New Construction credit combines enhanced MEP commissioning, enclosure commissioning and monitoring-based commissioning options, subject to its 4-point maximum. The enclosure route has its own review and field-testing evidence, including air-leakage and infrared thermography requirements.

Overall EAc5 maximum: 4 points, combining eligible commissioning options.

  • New Construction enclosure commissioning (Option 1, Path 2) is 1 point; the complete credit has a 4-point maximum
  • Review the full enclosure field-testing list, including water-related tests where applicable
  • Thermography does not substitute for air-leakage measurement or commissioning independence

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Envelope drawings, air-barrier continuity and construction details
  • Test boundary, floor area / volume and agreed test standards
  • Target leakage rate, test stage, access and temporary-sealing plan
  • Thermography conditions, inspection responsibilities and remediation process

How I support the assessment

  1. Coordinate the independent commissioning provider and enclosure process with the relevant ASTM route
  2. Agree enclosure test types, sampling, readiness and instrumentation / conditions
  3. Track field findings, corrective actions and final enclosure commissioning evidence

What I deliver

  • Test / inspection scope and readiness checklist
  • Airtightness and thermal-image findings linked to locations and details
  • Defect register, remedial-action review and retest evidence

Verification and responsibilities

Airtightness measurement and thermographic inspection are different activities. Site conditions, competent testing, remediation and close-out determine the final evidence quality.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
