BREEAM International NC V7.1

Water leak detection and prevention

Leak-detection strategy and controls review, including coordination with hydrant and sprinkler water systems where applicable.

Wat 03Up to 2 credits (non-residential)

What the requirement covers

Wat 03 distinguishes major-leak detection from water-flow control and, for residential projects, accessible leak-isolation provisions. The chosen building-type route and the water-supply boundary determine the evidence needed.

  • Leak detection and flow control / residential isolation are distinct requirements
  • Coordinate fire-protection supplies so life-safety functionality is not compromised
  • Final evidence should show installed locations and the specified detection / control capability

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Occupancy, operating days and sanitary-fixture schedules
  • Flow rates at the specified pressure and effective flush volumes
  • Potable and alternative-water schematics, meters and end-use boundaries
  • Applicable appliance data, water-reuse assumptions and final product sheets

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

How I support the assessment

  1. Map water entry, building distribution and the monitored boundaries
  2. Review alarm thresholds, abnormal-flow detection, communications and intended response
  3. Review WC-area demand controls or residential isolation valves, then coordinate installation and functional checks

What I deliver

  • Traceable water-use calculation or metering / leak-detection review
  • Fixture / system data register tied to drawings and specifications
  • Evidence checklist for installation, readings and handover

Verification and responsibilities

The calculation depends on the selected products, pressure assumptions and use profile. Installed product evidence and operating commitments remain with the design, construction and facilities teams.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
