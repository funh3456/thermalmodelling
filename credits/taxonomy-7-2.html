EU Taxonomy · 2021/2139 · 1 January 2026 consolidation

Renovation of existing buildings

Energy-performance assessment, including the applicable major-renovation route or primary-energy-demand reduction pathway.

7.2Technical screening criterion · no credit points

What the requirement covers

Annex I activity 7.2 allows the applicable major-renovation requirements or the primary-energy-demand reduction route. The latter requires at least 30% reduction, with the baseline, improvement and evidence established using the regulation’s calculation provisions.

  • The 30% route excludes reductions in net demand arising solely from renewable energy sources
  • Do not mix inconsistent before / after calculation methods
  • The applicable major-renovation route is an alternative, not automatically the same 30% test

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Building activity, location, national energy requirements and assessment date
  • Energy certificate, national calculation and supporting design / as-built data
  • Area, completion date, ownership boundary and relevant activity-specific records
  • DNSH documentation, climate-risk assessment and named evidence owners

Calculation and evidence plan

Renovation reduction route

For the eligible reduction route, reduction (%) = 100 × (PED_before − PED_after) / PED_before. Establish the baseline / improvement under the regulation’s provisions and do not count the excluded renewable-only reduction. The major-renovation compliance route is an alternative.

How I support the assessment

  1. Select the renovation route and document pre-renovation energy evidence
  2. Calculate the eligible improvement and reconcile the post-renovation design / EPC
  3. Document the completion sequence and coordinate remaining DNSH evidence

What I deliver

  • Activity-specific technical-screening calculation / review
  • Requirement-to-evidence matrix with explicit gaps and responsibilities
  • Supporting energy / building-performance documentation

Verification and responsibilities

Energy compliance alone does not establish full Taxonomy alignment. Other technical criteria, DNSH, minimum safeguards and applicable legal / reporting boundaries need their own evidence.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
