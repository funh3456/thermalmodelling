Certification scope/BREEAM NC V7/Ene 01
BREEAM International NC V7.1

Energy and carbon performance for regulated energy uses

Regulated heating, cooling and building-services energy and carbon assessment; model and design-option support.

Ene 01Up to 9 credits + 3 exemplary credits

What the requirement covers

Ene 01 evaluates regulated energy performance against a defined baseline using the BREEAM Energy Performance Ratio. The approved modelling route, local regulations, building type and assessment boundary must be fixed before comparisons are made. Exemplary performance has a separate low- and zero-carbon energy test.

  • Use the International V7.1 route rather than a UK or older-version rule
  • Separate regulated performance from realistic total operational consumption in Ene 02
  • Support any exemplary claim with the manual’s eligible LZC energy accounting

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Architectural plans or BIM geometry, areas and use types
  • Envelope constructions, glazing, shading and infiltration assumptions
  • HVAC plant, distribution, efficiencies and control sequences
  • Occupancy, lighting, equipment, operating hours and weather-file metadata

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

How I support the assessment

  1. Confirm the applicable baseline, approved modelling route and regulated end uses
  2. Create coordinated baseline and proposed models; reconcile geometry, services and calculation settings
  3. Report the required energy / carbon indicators and complete the BREEAM performance calculation

What I deliver

  • Assumptions register and model QA record
  • Baseline / proposed or scenario result tables by end use and fuel
  • Calculation narrative, sensitivity results and assessor-ready evidence index

Verification and responsibilities

The design team confirms the specification and operating assumptions. Model outputs support the submission; they do not guarantee measured in-use consumption or the final certification decision.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
