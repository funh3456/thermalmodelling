Certification scope/LEED v5 BD+C/EAc6
LEED v5 BD+C · New Construction · July 2026

Grid Interactive

Demand flexibility, controls, load shifting and grid-interactive building-services strategy support.

EAc6Up to 2 points

What the requirement covers

EAc6 offers several grid-interactive strategies, including energy storage, demand response, automated demand-side management and resilience. The project needs to document the selected options and their actual system / programme capability.

  • A modelled load shift is not equivalent to participation in a demand-response programme
  • Distinguish resilience from ordinary efficiency or time scheduling
  • Confirm option-specific technical and documentation requirements

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Installed-system schedule and served floor areas
  • Control schematics, points lists, sequences and set-points
  • Manufacturer documentation for the claimed control capability
  • Commissioning responsibilities, functional-test plans and final settings

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

How I support the assessment

  1. Choose the eligible option mix and identify the controlled loads and critical systems
  2. Review storage, programme participation and automated control sequences
  3. Document final equipment, enablement, performance / programme commitments and the relevant option evidence

What I deliver

  • System-by-system applicability and capability matrix
  • Control-sequence review with evidence gaps and coordination comments
  • Design-to-as-built traceability and functional-test checklist

Verification and responsibilities

A design statement must be supported by the installed equipment and enabled controls. The contractor / commissioning team verifies operation; assessor or certification-provider approval is separate.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
