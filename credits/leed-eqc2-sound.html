EQc2Selected sound-environment option: 1–2 pointsOption 4 — Sound Environment

What the requirement covers

EQc2 Option 4 addresses the sound environment. It separates acoustic-expectation mapping from the acoustic-criteria route, with credit scope extending beyond building-services noise.

  • This page covers the 1–2 point sound-environment option
  • Noise, sound insulation and reverberation are different performance dimensions
  • Mechanical-noise calculations alone cannot establish every acoustic requirement

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Room uses, sensitive receivers, adjacency and façade details
  • Plant sound-power spectra, airflow, operating cases and duct paths
  • Acoustic criteria, room finishes and attenuation proposals
  • Specialist appointment, test locations and handover programme

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

FieldData / unitsEngineering check
Source dataSound-power spectrum; operating dutyDo not substitute an unqualified sound-pressure rating for sound power.
Receiver criteriaRoom use; agreed ambient-noise criterionUse the applicable building-type criteria and specialist brief.
Transmission pathDucts; attenuation; distance; barriersTrack breakout, regenerated noise and structure-borne paths where relevant.
Room propertiesVolume; absorption; finishesCoordinate architectural acoustic inputs with the appointed specialist.
Final evidenceTest method; locations; conditionsLink measurements, corrective works and repeated checks to the selected criteria.

These are engineering explanations and evidence-planning fields. Scheme-specific equations, acceptance limits, tables and exemptions are in the official references.

How I support the assessment

  1. Map indoor / outdoor acoustic expectations and the selected route
  2. Review mechanical-noise sources, sensitive receivers and design criteria with the acoustic team
  3. Prepare the selected-path narrative and coordinate the relevant calculations / measurement evidence

What I deliver

  • Mechanical-noise / attenuation calculations and design comments
  • Room / plant criteria matrix with the applicable assessment route
  • Evidence and measurement coordination checklist

Verification and responsibilities

Mechanical-noise support does not replace a suitably qualified acoustician, whole-building sound-insulation / reverberation design, or required completed-building measurements.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
