BREEAM International NC V7.1

Airtightness & thermography

Building-envelope airtightness testing and thermographic diagnostics to support fabric-performance verification and model-input QA where applicable to the selected BREEAM route.

SITE QA1 credit within Man 04

What the requirement covers

This service supports the envelope-testing route within BREEAM Man 04; it is not an additional stand-alone issue. Both airtightness testing and thermographic inspection support fabric integrity, followed by remediation and final close-out.

  • The service belongs to the Man 04 envelope-testing credit
  • Interpret images against temperature differences, weather and construction details
  • Retain competent-professional reports and records of remediation

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Envelope drawings, air-barrier continuity and construction details
  • Test boundary, floor area / volume and agreed test standards
  • Target leakage rate, test stage, access and temporary-sealing plan
  • Thermography conditions, inspection responsibilities and remediation process

Calculation and evidence plan

Airtightness metrics

Where the chosen test standard / project uses these metrics, n₅₀ = measured air flow at 50 Pa / internal volume (h⁻¹), while q₅₀ = that air flow / the specified envelope area (m³/h·m²). Record the actual reference pressure and boundary; use the accepted scheme / test-standard definition.

How I support the assessment

  1. Agree the air-barrier boundary, test standards, test readiness and thermography conditions
  2. Carry out / coordinate competent testing and locate leakage or thermal irregularities
  3. Document corrective work and the verification of closed defects before handover

What I deliver

  • Test / inspection scope and readiness checklist
  • Airtightness and thermal-image findings linked to locations and details
  • Defect register, remedial-action review and retest evidence

Verification and responsibilities

Airtightness measurement and thermographic inspection are different activities. Site conditions, competent testing, remediation and close-out determine the final evidence quality.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
