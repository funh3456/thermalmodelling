Lighting & demand controls

Internal and external lighting efficiency, lighting controls, skylight-related inputs and CO-based parking ventilation strategies.

EEM22–EEM26Efficiency measure · no credit points

What the requirement covers

EEM22–EEM26 covers internal / external lighting, lighting controls, skylights and CO-based parking ventilation. The group combines electrical, daylight-related and mechanical measures with separate calculation boundaries.

  • Distinguish internal and external lighting power / areas
  • CO-based parking ventilation is different from CO₂-based occupied-space ventilation
  • Check the interactions between skylights, heat gains and lighting savings

What I deliver

  • Audit submission support and as-built reconciliation checklist

Verification and responsibilities

EDGE calculates performance against its own baseline. A selected measure is not a credit point or a certificate; auditor evidence and the certification provider’s decision remain separate.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
