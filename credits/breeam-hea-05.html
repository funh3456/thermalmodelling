BREEAM International NC V7.1

Thermal Comfort

Full dynamic thermal analysis, overheating assessment, PMV / PPD and future-climate scenarios.

Hea 05Up to 3 credits

What the requirement covers

Hea 05 has separate current-climate, future-climate and zoning / controls routes. The current-climate model uses an applicable approved comfort standard and relevant occupied spaces. Future-climate analysis and control strategy build on the current-climate assessment.

The current-climate modelling route is 1 credit; the complete issue can provide 3.

  • One credit each: current climate, future climate, and zoning / controls; applicability can reduce the total
  • M1.3 distinguishes the future-climate scenario and horizon by conditioning strategy
  • Industrial storage / operational areas have specific applicability notes; do not apply office criteria indiscriminately

Source: official requirements listed below.

Future-climate route

International V7.1 M1.3 specifies the scenario, percentile and horizon by conditioning strategy: naturally ventilated buildings use a 50-year horizon with RCP 4.5 or SSP2-4.5; mechanically ventilated / mixed-mode buildings use a 15-year horizon with RCP 8.5 or SSP5-8.5. The specified percentile is 50%. Retain provenance of the future-weather generation and project completion date.

Source: official Hea 05 technical manual, methodology M1.3.

What the project needs to provide

  • Room geometry, façade orientation, glazing, shading and construction data
  • Occupancy, activity, clothing, internal gains and occupied-hour schedules
  • Ventilation strategy, window operation, HVAC set-points and control logic
  • Current and, where required, projected weather files with scenario and period metadata

Calculation and evidence plan

Comfort analysis

PMV / PPD evaluation needs air and mean radiant temperature, air speed, humidity, clothing and activity assumptions. An adaptive method instead uses its own conditioning / outdoor-temperature applicability and criteria. Report occupied-hour room results and keep current and future climates separate.

How I support the assessment

  1. Agree relevant occupied spaces, conditioning mode and the approved comfort / overheating method
  2. Run current and applicable projected climates, retaining scenario, percentile and time-horizon provenance
  3. Use the results to refine passive measures, thermal zoning, user controls and system interlocks

What I deliver

  • Room-by-room comfort and overheating results for the selected assessment route
  • Assumptions, weather-file provenance and model QA record
  • Design-option comparison and recommended zoning / control changes

Verification and responsibilities

The project must agree the relevant spaces and assessment method. Modelling is distinct from occupied-building measurements, operational policies and certification review.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
