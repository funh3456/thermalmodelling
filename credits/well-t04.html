WELL v2 · Q2 2025

Individual Thermal Control

Personal thermal-control and comfort-design support, including modelling of thermal conditions where useful.

T04Up to 3 points

What the requirement covers

T04 supports individual thermal control. The selected parts address occupant access to personal cooling / heating or local control and related operational provisions; modelling can inform suitability but the provision itself must be evidenced.

  • Select parts and project type before assuming the maximum points
  • Personal devices, access arrangements and workplace policies are not replaced by a PMV calculation
  • Review thermal-control provisions with the owner / facilities team

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Room geometry, façade orientation, glazing, shading and construction data
  • Occupancy, activity, clothing, internal gains and occupied-hour schedules
  • Ventilation strategy, window operation, HVAC set-points and control logic
  • Current and, where required, projected weather files with scenario and period metadata

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

FieldData / unitsEngineering check
Thermal environmentAir / radiant / operative temperature; °CDocument the chosen comfort method and the relevant occupied-space boundary.
Personal parametersMetabolic rate; clothing insulationUse agreed occupancy/activity and seasonal clothing assumptions.
Other comfort inputsAir speed; relative humidityUse a complete input set when PMV / PPD is the selected method.
Occupied periodsHours / year; room useDo not dilute occupied-hour failures with unoccupied periods.

ResultsPMV; PPD; overheating hoursEvaluate each applicable criterion rather than reporting one temperature average.

These are engineering explanations and evidence-planning fields. Scheme-specific equations, acceptance limits, tables and exemptions are in the official references.

How I support the assessment

  1. Define occupant groups, relevant areas and selected T04 parts
  2. Review available local / personal controls and thermal conditions
  3. Coordinate procurement, access policies and the applicable design / assurance records

What I deliver

  • Room-by-room comfort and overheating results for the selected assessment route
  • Assumptions, weather-file provenance and model QA record
  • Design-option comparison and recommended zoning / control changes

Verification and responsibilities

The project must agree the relevant spaces and assessment method. Modelling is distinct from occupied-building measurements, operational policies and certification review.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
