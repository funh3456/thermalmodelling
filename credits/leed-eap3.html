Fundamental Commissioning

MEP commissioning-plan, design-review and documentation support; independent CxP and field verification requirements apply.

EAp3Required prerequisite

What the requirement covers

EAp3 establishes fundamental commissioning duties for the relevant building systems. Owner requirements, basis of design, an appointed commissioning provider, reviews, testing and close-out must be coordinated.

  • A document package alone does not replace the commissioning-provider role
  • Appointments and early review timing matter
  • State separately which reviews, site tests and witness activities are in the proposed service scope

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Owner requirements, basis of design and coordinated system designs
  • Commissioning scope, programme, procurement route and named responsibilities
  • Specifications, submittals, control sequences and test plans
  • Training, user-guide, issues-log and handover arrangements

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

How I support the assessment

  1. Confirm commissioning-provider eligibility, independence and system coverage
  2. Review owner requirements, basis of design, commissioning plan and specifications
  3. Coordinate field verification, issue closure and final commissioning documentation

What I deliver

  • Design-stage commissioning review and responsibility matrix
  • Commissioning-plan / evidence review with issues and close-out actions
  • Handover-document and training checklist

Verification and responsibilities

Independent commissioning roles, site attendance, witnessing and testing must be appointed and scoped explicitly. Document review alone does not complete commissioning.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
