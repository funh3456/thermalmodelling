Water-efficient fixtures & appliances

Design-stage water-use inputs and documentation for showers, faucets, WCs, bidets, urinals, kitchen fittings and applicable appliances.

WEM01/02/04/06–11Efficiency measure · no credit points

What the requirement covers

This group covers the listed efficient showers, taps, toilets, bidets, urinals and applicable kitchen / laundry appliances. Measure IDs intentionally skip some numbers; only the current guide’s eligible measures and project typology apply.

  • The listed IDs are WEM01, 02, 04 and 06–11, not a continuous WEM01–11 range
  • Rated product consumption and operating assumptions have different evidence bases
  • Keep kitchen appliances and domestic sanitary fixtures in their correct categories

  • EDGE project location, building typology and registered software / guide version
  • Design-tab inputs, areas, use assumptions and selected efficiency measures
  • Product performance data, system schematics and proposed quantities
  • Auditor evidence requirements and construction / installation records

How I support the assessment

  1. Compile the fixture / appliance schedule by eligible measure ID
  2. Verify flow at the specified pressure, flush volume, consumption per cycle and covered quantities
  3. Compare EDGE scenarios and reconcile installed products with the evidence matrix

What I deliver

  • EDGE input register and measure-by-measure evidence matrix
  • Design-option comparison and impact on the applicable EDGE savings result
  • Audit submission support and as-built reconciliation checklist

Verification and responsibilities

EDGE calculates performance against its own baseline. A selected measure is not a credit point or a certificate; auditor evidence and the certification provider’s decision remain separate.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
