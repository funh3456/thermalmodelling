Commissioning and handover

Design-stage commissioning review, commissioning documentation and technical handover support; verification can require targeted attendance.

Man 04Up to 4 credits

What the requirement covers

Man 04 covers commissioning schedules and responsibilities, a specialist commissioning role, envelope testing and handover. Early appointments and a realistic contractor programme are part of the evidence, not just final reports.

  • Four routes: schedule / responsibilities, commissioning role, envelope testing and handover
  • Envelope testing includes airtightness and a thermographic survey plus defect close-out
  • Distinguish design review from independent specialist appointment and field witnessing

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Owner requirements, basis of design and coordinated system designs
  • Commissioning scope, programme, procurement route and named responsibilities
  • Specifications, submittals, control sequences and test plans
  • Training, user-guide, issues-log and handover arrangements

How I support the assessment

  1. Define the commissioning scope, standards, appointments and programme before technical design where required
  2. Track design / submittal reviews, testing, BMS checks and issue closure
  3. Coordinate building-user guides, training and relevant fabric-testing evidence

What I deliver

  • Design-stage commissioning review and responsibility matrix
  • Commissioning-plan / evidence review with issues and close-out actions
  • Handover-document and training checklist

Verification and responsibilities

Independent commissioning roles, site attendance, witnessing and testing must be appointed and scoped explicitly. Document review alone does not complete commissioning.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
