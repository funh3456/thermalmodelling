WELL v2 · Q2 2025

Humidity Control

Humidity-control design and dynamic humidity analysis where the selected WELL pathway accepts modelling evidence.

T071 point

What the requirement covers

T07 addresses humidity control through the selected accepted route. Mechanical capability, a permitted modelling route, or another approved evidence pathway must match the project’s applicable WELL version and selected part.

  • The cited mechanical-control requirement maintains 30–60% RH in relevant areas, subject to its exclusions
  • A modelling route has its own occupied-hour acceptance test; do not transfer a threshold from another version
  • Exclude / treat high-humidity areas exactly as the selected part permits

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Room geometry, façade orientation, glazing, shading and construction data
  • Occupancy, activity, clothing, internal gains and occupied-hour schedules
  • Ventilation strategy, window operation, HVAC set-points and control logic
  • Current and, where required, projected weather files with scenario and period metadata

Calculation and evidence plan

Latent-load and humidity analysis

Model moisture generation, outdoor-air humidity, ventilation schedules, plant dehumidification / humidification and any condensation constraints. Assess the space-by-space occupied periods against the selected accepted route. The cited mechanical capability route is a separate equipment-design requirement.

How I support the assessment

  1. Agree the humidity range, relevant spaces and accepted compliance route
  2. Model latent gains, ventilation, plant capacity and occupied-hour humidity where that route is selected
  3. Document the humidity-control design and required assurance / monitoring evidence

What I deliver

  • Room-by-room comfort and overheating results for the selected assessment route
  • Assumptions, weather-file provenance and model QA record
  • Design-option comparison and recommended zoning / control changes

Verification and responsibilities

The project must agree the relevant spaces and assessment method. Modelling is distinct from occupied-building measurements, operational policies and certification review.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
