Climate risk & vulnerability assessment

Physical climate-risk screening, future-climate scenarios and adaptation-measure assessment for building-performance risks.

DNSHDNSH requirement — no credit points

What the requirement covers

Annex I Appendix A sets the generic DNSH climate-adaptation approach: identify material physical hazards, assess vulnerability and select adaptation solutions. Scenario choice, asset lifetime, resolution and implementation evidence are integral to the assessment.

  • For major investments, the prescribed approach includes at least 10–30-year climate projections
  • Heat-performance modelling is one input within the wider hazard assessment
  • Adaptation must not transfer harm to people, nature, other assets or activities

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Site coordinates, surroundings, building use and expected asset life
  • Physical-climate hazard screening and authoritative climate datasets
  • Scenario range, time horizons, spatial resolution and uncertainty
  • Existing adaptation measures and design / operational responsibilities

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

How I support the assessment

  1. Screen the Appendix A hazards against the asset and expected lifetime
  2. Assess material risks with appropriate current / future scenarios and performance models
  3. Document adaptation measures, implementation ownership and residual-risk evidence

What I deliver

  • Hazard screening and materiality / vulnerability register
  • Scenario and data-provenance record with performance analyses where relevant
  • Adaptation recommendations, action ownership and evidence plan

Verification and responsibilities

Thermal modelling addresses heat-related performance; a complete climate-risk assessment may also need flood, wind, wildfire, coastal, geotechnical or other specialist inputs.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
