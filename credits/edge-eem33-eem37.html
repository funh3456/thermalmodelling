Renewables, additional savings & refrigerants

On-site and off-site renewable energy, additional energy-saving measures, carbon-offset inputs and low-impact refrigerant assessment.

EEM33–EEM37Efficiency measure · no credit points

What the requirement covers

EEM33–EEM37 groups renewables, additional energy-saving measures, off-site procurement, carbon offsets and low-impact refrigerants. These mechanisms have different accounting and evidence rules; some are conditional on the certification level or approved route.

  • Offsets and renewable procurement are not equivalent to reducing building energy demand
  • An additional-saving claim needs an accepted calculation / evidence route
  • Check the current guide and certification protocol before assuming an EEM contributes to the same savings target

How I support the assessment

  1. Define the selected mechanism, generation / contract boundary and accounting period
  2. Calculate documented energy or emissions impacts without double counting
  3. Coordinate procurement, exceptional-saving approval and refrigerant evidence with the EDGE auditor

What I deliver

  • EDGE input register and measure-by-measure evidence matrix
  • Design-option comparison and impact on the applicable EDGE savings result
  • Audit submission support and as-built reconciliation checklist

Verification and responsibilities

EDGE calculates performance against its own baseline. A selected measure is not a credit point or a certificate; auditor evidence and the certification provider’s decision remain separate.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
