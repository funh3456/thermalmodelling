Certification scope/BREEAM NC V7/Pol 01
BREEAM International NC V7.1

Impact of refrigerants

Refrigerant inventory, GWP / impact calculations and leak-detection design review based on project and manufacturer data.

Pol 01Up to 3 credits

What the requirement covers

Pol 01 evaluates refrigerant impact, system eligibility, safety, ozone depletion, direct life-cycle emissions and leak-control measures. The issue has a no-refrigerant route and routes for systems that use refrigerants; the selected route must be supported by a complete system inventory.

  • A low-GWP product name is insufficient without capacity / charge and route evidence
  • Relevant electric-compressor systems have safety and zero-ODP prerequisites
  • Detection and containment functionality need their own design and final installation records

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Complete refrigerant-system inventory, including relevant tenant / process systems
  • Refrigerant designation, charge, GWP and ODP values with their source
  • Cooling / heating capacity, leak-control details and replacement assumptions
  • Manufacturer information and commissioning / maintenance records

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

These are engineering explanations and evidence-planning fields. Scheme-specific equations, acceptance limits, tables and exemptions are in the official references.

How I support the assessment

  1. Fix the assessed refrigerant-system boundary and collect charge, capacity, GWP and ODP data
  2. Apply the BREEAM direct-effect life-cycle calculation and test the applicable impact route
  3. Review automatic detection, isolation / containment and manufacturer installation evidence

What I deliver

  • Refrigerant inventory and auditable impact calculations
  • Options comparison with safety and technical constraints identified
  • Leak-detection / containment review and evidence register

Verification and responsibilities

Low GWP is one part of the assessment. Safety, leakage, charge, use boundaries and the selected scheme methodology also matter. Installation checks and certification approval are separate.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
