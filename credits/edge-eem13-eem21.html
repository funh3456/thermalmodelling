HVAC, heating, DHW & ventilation

Cooling and heating efficiency, variable-speed drives, fresh-air pre-conditioning, room controls, domestic hot water, economizers and demand-controlled ventilation.

EEM13–EEM21Efficiency measure · no credit points

What the requirement covers

EEM13–EEM21 covers cooling and heating efficiency, variable-speed drives, fresh-air pre-conditioning, room heating controls, hot water, economizers and demand-controlled ventilation. The actual system configuration determines relevant measure inputs.

  • Nameplate values need the correct rating / seasonal-efficiency basis
  • Demand-controlled ventilation, economizers and VSDs are distinct measures
  • External dynamic modelling can support options but does not replace EDGE input / audit requirements

How I support the assessment

  1. Reconcile plant, distribution and control arrangements with the EDGE model
  2. Collect efficiency / capacity data at the guide’s required rating conditions
  3. Document each selected measure’s technology, control capability and installed evidence

What I deliver

  • EDGE input register and measure-by-measure evidence matrix
  • Design-option comparison and impact on the applicable EDGE savings result
  • Audit submission support and as-built reconciliation checklist

Verification and responsibilities

EDGE calculates performance against its own baseline. A selected measure is not a credit point or a certificate; auditor evidence and the certification provider’s decision remain separate.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
