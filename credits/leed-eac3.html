LEED v5 BD+C · New Construction · July 2026

Enhanced Energy Efficiency

Whole-building energy simulation aligned with the applicable ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G methodology.

EAc3Up to 10 pointsEnergy Simulation pathway

What the requirement covers

EAc3 Option 2 evaluates whole-building performance using future source energy under ASHRAE Appendix G. It uses the edition adopted for EAp2 and the v5 performance-factor / index rules, with explicit treatment of plug and process savings and renewable contributions.

  • The July 2026 requirements replace cost-based comparisons with future source energy
  • Use project-applicable electricity conversion factors and v5 building-performance factors
  • Report renewable and plug / process contributions transparently rather than masking them in a single total

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Architectural plans or BIM geometry, areas and use types
  • Envelope constructions, glazing, shading and infiltration assumptions
  • HVAC plant, distribution, efficiencies and control sequences
  • Occupancy, lighting, equipment, operating hours and weather-file metadata

Calculation and evidence plan

Future source-energy performance

July 2026 EAc3 Option 2 uses PI = proposed future source energy / baseline future source energy and a target containing the applicable building-performance factor. Apply the v5 equations and factors, distinguish regulated / unregulated energy, and report the prescribed renewable and process-load variants.

How I support the assessment

  1. Reconcile the EAp2 baseline, proposed model and mandatory provisions
  2. Calculate future source energy and performance indices with / without selected contributions
  3. Apply the v5 New Construction point tables and document exceptional calculations

What I deliver

  • Assumptions register and model QA record
  • Baseline / proposed or scenario result tables by end use and fuel
  • Calculation narrative, sensitivity results and assessor-ready evidence index

Verification and responsibilities

The design team confirms the specification and operating assumptions. Model outputs support the submission; they do not guarantee measured in-use consumption or the final certification decision.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
