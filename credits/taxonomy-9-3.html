Energy-performance services

Building energy simulation and related engineering analysis supporting professional energy-performance services.

9.3Technical screening criterion · no credit points

What the requirement covers

Annex I activity 9.3 covers specified professional services related to building energy performance, including energy simulations, consultations, accredited audits / performance assessments, energy-management services and other listed activities. The service activity and its applicable DNSH evidence must be identified.

  • This is a professional-services activity, not a building certificate or credit
  • An energy simulation does not establish the client building’s full Taxonomy alignment
  • Keep service eligibility distinct from activity 7.1 / 7.2 / 7.7 technical tests

Source: official requirements listed below.

What the project needs to provide

  • Building activity, location, national energy requirements and assessment date
  • Energy certificate, national calculation and supporting design / as-built data
  • Area, completion date, ownership boundary and relevant activity-specific records
  • DNSH documentation, climate-risk assessment and named evidence owners

Calculation and evidence plan

Use the selected requirement route to fix the assessment boundary and evidence basis before preparing calculations. The following fields make the proposed engineering review traceable.

How I support the assessment

  1. Map the contracted service to the listed activity and energy-performance objective
  2. Prepare traceable calculation / consultation outputs and document qualifications where relevant
  3. Record the service boundary and applicable climate-adaptation evidence

What I deliver

  • Activity-specific technical-screening calculation / review
  • Requirement-to-evidence matrix with explicit gaps and responsibilities
  • Supporting energy / building-performance documentation

Verification and responsibilities

Energy compliance alone does not establish full Taxonomy alignment. Other technical criteria, DNSH, minimum safeguards and applicable legal / reporting boundaries need their own evidence.

Before work starts, agree the assessment boundary, selected route, current design issue and named owner of each missing evidence item. At handover, reconcile the calculations with final products, installed systems and any required test records.
